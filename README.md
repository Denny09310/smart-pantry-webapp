# Smart Pantry — never let food expire unnoticed again

Smart Pantry is your household's shared food inventory. Install it once on
your home network and the whole family shares one always-up-to-date pantry —
on every phone, tablet, and computer, no accounts, no passwords, no cloud.

## Why you'll love it

- **See everything at a glance.** Every item, quantity, location, and expiry
  date in one place — color-coded by urgency so expiring food shouts first.
- **Get reminded before food goes bad.** A daily check flags what's expiring
  and nudges opted-in devices with push notifications, even with the app closed.
- **Add items in seconds.** Scan the barcode with your camera and the name,
  brand, and size fill in themselves. Or type it — your choice.
- **Everyone knows who added what.** Each person picks their name; every item
  shows who brought it home. No sign-in screens, ever.
- **Installs like a real app.** Add it to your home screen from the browser
  and it works like a native app — including offline access to your pantry.
- **Your data stays yours.** It runs on your own network, not in someone
  else's cloud. No tracking, no subscriptions, no ads.

## On the horizon

- **Use it up, don't throw it out.** Smart Pantry will soon suggest recipes
  for whatever is about to expire — dinner ideas from your own shelves,
  powered by TheMealDB.

## Made for real kitchens

- Fridge, freezer, pantry, and cabinet locations out of the box
- Expiry badges: expired, today, expiring soon, fresh
- Works on desktop and mobile, in light and dark company
- Updates install themselves with a single tap — never lose your list

Smart Pantry: buy what you need, use what you have, waste nothing.
